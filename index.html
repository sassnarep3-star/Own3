How to choose wisely

We won’t rank what we can’t substantiate.

Before recommending a competition, we need official confirmation of a genuinely free entry path, prize terms, active dates, geographic and age eligibility, payout mechanics, and time/skill demands. Expected value also requires odds or field size. Those inputs are currently unverified, so the dashboard makes no “best bet” or earnings promise.
