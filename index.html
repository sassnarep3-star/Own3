What the audit changed

23 rows do not pay money at all. Their own prize field describes merchandise, tickets, vouchers, in-game items or points. They sit in a ledger titled "free competitions that pay real money". Every one is flagged NO_CASH_PRIZE.

9 rows are not competitions. Seven pool-hosting tools ("prizes decided by your group") and two directories of other contests.

12 rows have no official source. The project's own rule says a stated prize figure must cite the operator's own page. These rows cite only affiliate or forum pages — and 4 of them are still marked live.

13 more rows mislabel a source. They grade a press-release wire, a social post or an affiliate site as official.

35 rows state no dollar figure at all, and 21 rows admit in their own text that the prize is unconfirmed, paused or not running. Those values stay blank on this site.
