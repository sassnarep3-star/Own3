# Independent publication audit — 2026-11-02

## Executive summary

The [reference ledger](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/FreeMoneyApps/) reports 206 listings and presents a long scrollable catalog with no search or filters. Its scope includes free practice paths that do not pay cash, so inclusion alone is not evidence of a free-entry cash prize. The [published dashboard](https://sassnarep3-star.github.io/Own3/) reports 206 candidates, of which 26 had official pages checked and 180 remained unverified as of 2026-11-01. The [review page](https://sassnarep3-star.github.io/Own3/flagged-for-review.html) documents exclusions and other issues. Those counts are not a fresh verification of all 206 entries.

The existing static site and both archived builds passed their local validators on 2026-11-02. These check generated output, internal links and URL shape; they do **not** establish that external links work or that every cited value is correct. This audit sampled the following pages, not all rows.

## Scope and limitations

The source pages above were read on 2026-11-02. This is a small independent sample, not line-by-line verification of the catalog. Competition availability, deadlines, payout terms, geographic eligibility, AI policies and external links can change; consult the current operator rules before investing time. No expected value or winning probability was established.
