Executive summary

Data as of 2026-11-02. Every figure below comes from data/competitions.json / data/stats.json; values not confirmed on an official page are blank, never estimated.

The short version

  • The Free Money & Contest Ledger (source site) lists 206 free-entry cash competitions. It was re-checked against operators' own pages: 27 rows (13%) were read on an official page, 6 fully verified, 11 partially, 10 contradicted by the operator's own page. 179 rows remain unverified and are shown blank.
  • Do not trust the ledger's headline numbers. Its "189 true free" count includes 26 ended and 10 cautioned rows, and at least one "free" contest (Tom Howard poetry) charges a $27 fee.
  • 14 rows have no usable official source and are held out of the tables (see Flagged for Review). The other 192 rows each carry an official link.
  • The best verified opportunities for an AI-assisted entrant are in Best bets: 9 Tier A (free entry confirmed) and 4 Tier B (large prize, entry terms still to read).

What to do first

  1. Open Best bets and start with Tier A; check each competition's rules before entering.
  2. Time-sensitive: FundedFast championship sign-ups run 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-18 (operator changed the deadline between 11-01 and 11-02; recheck); NASCAR Fantasy Live closes 2026-11-08; Sony World Photography Student category closes 2026-11-27; ESPN College Pick'em ends 2026-12-06.
  3. AI-agent rule check: the only AI rule read on an official page is Winning Writers' ban on AI-generated text (Wergle Flomp). All other competitions' AI-tool policies are unverified.
