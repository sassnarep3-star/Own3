Dashboard / Executive summary

Executive summary

An audited replacement for the FreeMoneyApps ledger: 206 competitions, rebuilt as a mobile-first dashboard with per-category tables, a verification level on every row, and a published list of every defect found. Prepared 2026-11-01.

Purpose

The existing site (FreeMoneyApps) is a strong piece of research with a bad interface: 206 entries rendered as one long undifferentiated scroll, no search, no filters, heavy external font and CSS dependencies, and prize figures that are not always traceable to an operator's own page. The goal was a site that answers one question quickly — which free competitions are worth my time, and what will they actually pay? — and that never states a number it cannot source.

Six findings that change how you should use the list

  1. 23 rows do not pay money. Their own prize fields describe merchandise, tickets, vouchers, in-game items or points. They are honestly labelled row by row, but they sit in a ledger whose stated purpose is cash, so the headline count oversells what is on offer.
  2. 9 rows are not competitions. Seven pool-hosting tools, whose own prize field reads "prizes decided by your group", and two directories of other contests.
  3. 12 rows break the project's own sourcing rule, which requires a stated prize figure to cite the operator's own page. They rest on affiliate, forum or social sources — and some are still marked live.
  4. 13 further sources are mislabelled official — press-release wires, a broker review aggregator, affiliate bonus blogs, club fan sites and X posts. Three live rows quote exact prize tables with no operator page behind them.
  5. The 19-row trading category is a category error. Broker demo contests are customer-acquisition promotions; prizes are often credited as trading bonuses or prop accounts. The upstream file excluded bookmaker and DFS funnels on exactly this logic but kept these.
  6. Status decays. Status is inherited from a 2026-08-19 snapshot rather than observed. MLB Beat the Streak is marked live although its own rules close entry at the end of the 2026 season, which has finished. Every row here shows its review date for this reason.

Recommendation

Concentrate effort on Tech & data and Forecasting. These are the only categories that pass all six tests that matter for agent-assisted entry: automated scoring, a public leaderboard, text-readable rules, free entry with no rake, an outcome that depends on skill rather than luck, and a prize paid as cash. Kaggle, DrivenData, ARC Prize and Metaculus were each opened and confirmed during this audit.

Treat Fantasy & sports picks as low-effort entertainment with a handful of genuinely free prizes — CBS Pro Pick'em was verified as free with 18 × $1,000 weekly prizes, a $5,000 season prize and a $100,000 perfect-week prize — but it is seasonal and luck-dominated.

Deprioritise Trading and Skill despite their larger headline numbers, because the fee, the rake or the credit terms absorb the expected value.

What could not be confirmed

Stated plainly, because the value of this exercise depends on it: only 9 of 206 rows were verified against the operator's own page. The remaining 151 rows that carry an official source are as-reported from the upstream snapshot — sourced, but not re-opened. Link rot, changed prize structures and expired eligibility are all possible on any row not marked verified. Nothing has been inferred or back-filled to paper over those gaps: where a figure could not be sourced, the cell is blank and the row is flagged on Flagged for review.

Outbound network access from the build environment was limited to a host allowlist, so a several-hundred-link crawl was not possible. The structural checks confirm every URL is well-formed and points where the row claims; they cannot confirm every destination is still live.

Next steps

  1. Re-open the 151 "Official source" rows a batch at a time and promote them to verified, or flag what has moved.
  2. Replace the affiliate-only evidence on the three live trading rows with the broker's own contest terms, or blank their prize tables.
  3. Re-poll status on the 129 seasonal rows at the start of each season rather than displaying an inherited value.
  4. Split 99designs and Freelancer.com out of "creative contests" into a "client-funded work" view.
  5. Add a change log so a reader can see when a prize figure or eligibility rule last moved.
