How a flag can be cleared

  1. Link the operator’s current official rules page for the exact contest.
  2. Verify the entry is free on the actual prize path—not merely that the app or practice mode is free.
  3. Record current dates, exact prize form/value, age and geographic limits, payout and tax/claim conditions.
  4. Confirm the rules link works and date-stamp a fresh review. If a fact is absent or ambiguous, leave it blank and retain the flag.
