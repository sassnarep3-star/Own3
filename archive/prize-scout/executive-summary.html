Executive summary

The referenced ledger is a useful discovery index, but it is not a dependable shortlist for choosing where to spend time. The safest rebuild is a transparent candidate register that preserves the 206 lead-catalog entries while withholding all unverified financial and eligibility claims.

Recommendation

Do not prioritize a competition on headline prize size alone. First confirm its current official rules, truly free entry path, open dates, eligibility, payout terms and realistic time demand. Until then, no entry is ranked or recommended.

What we reviewed

  • The published reference page, Free Money & Contest Ledger.
  • Its public source repository and candidate data file, pinned to commit 466f7c1e7a14b52b4a0d36beb84a00d68fd409b7; the source file lists 206 records in 9 categories.
  • The repository’s dated audit notes, including AUDIT_2026-08-19.md, and its explicitly superseded 100_VERIFIED_CONTESTS.md.

Every row’s prize, entry cost, eligibility, availability, payout, effort and expected-value fields are intentionally blank. Candidate names and category labels are carried over as leads, not certified facts. Each row links to a URL the lead dataset labels as official, or to an operator-site starting point where it did not supply such a source. The classification of those links and the current contents have not been independently checked here.

Important findings from the source audit

The upstream audit itself reports several material issues, including a USA TODAY contest URL that redirects, a Football Nation page that does not show the advertised pick’em/prize, and a Pickwatch entry marked live despite the referenced page saying the contest was not running for 2026. It also flags ended contests marked live, season-old fantasy league codes, disputed prize values, eligibility errors and inconsistencies between the repository’s documents. These are reported by the upstream audit; this build has not independently re-tested those third-party pages. See the Flagged for Review page for linked leads and context.

How to determine the best use of time

  1. Eligibility first: confirm age, country/state, residency, account and any skill or identity requirements in the official rules.
  2. Free-entry test: confirm that no purchase, deposit, paid entry, subscription or wagering is required for the cash-prize path. A free app or free practice mode is not necessarily a free cash competition.
  3. Prize and timing: verify current dates, prize table, number of prizes, tie rules and the exact award form (cash versus points, gift cards, credits or bonus funds).
  4. Effort and odds: record time to enter/play, skill fit, field size and credible odds where available. Do not fabricate expected value from a headline prize.
  5. Payout risk: check claims windows, tax documentation, withdrawal thresholds, payment methods and restrictions.

Only after these checks can the site provide a meaningful, evidence-based comparison. Until then, the catalog is for research—not financial advice, a guarantee of winnings, or an endorsement.
