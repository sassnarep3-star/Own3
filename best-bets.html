Best bets

A dated research shortlist (last refreshed 2026-11-02), using only facts read from official pages. Recheck deadlines and terms before entering. This is a shortlist, not a promise of winnings.

How these were chosen

  1. Only rows whose OFFICIAL page was read on 2026-11-01 or rechecked on 2026-11-02 are eligible (verification level verified or partial). Unverified rows are never ranked.
  2. The official page must show the contest as open (or opening within days) and show a cash prize figure.
  3. Tier A = free entry is explicitly stated on the official page. Tier B = prize and open status confirmed, but the page read did not state the entry fee. Tier C = free entry confirmed but prize or open status not confirmed.
  4. Inside a tier, rows are ordered by the largest prize figure verified on the official page (field headlineUsd in data/bestbets.json). Headline prize is NOT expected value: field sizes and odds were not published on the pages read, so no win probability is claimed.

Cautions that apply to every row

  • Prizes are headline figures. Win odds, field sizes, tax withholding and payout timing were not on the pages read.
  • AI-agent use: only one official rule was read that addresses AI — Winning Writers (Wergle Flomp) forbids AI-generated text. Every other competition's AI-tool policy is unverified; check the rules before using an agent.
  • Tax treatment, payout timing and any ID or KYC requirements were not reviewed for most rows. Read each operator's rules.
  • Rows whose official page shows the contest ended, closed or charges a fee (e.g. Tom Howard poetry, EWC Play, TradingView Leap) are excluded from this list. Where only the figures differed from the old ledger (CheckMate, FundedFast), the official page's figures are used. See Flagged for Review.
